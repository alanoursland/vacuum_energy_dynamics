# 7. The Model Defends Itself: Self-Tests and Closures

**Status: outline (no prose yet).**

## Content outline
- THE EVENNESS THEOREM: any real, finite-range harmonic action on any
  graph has D(-k) = D(k)* => spectrum exactly even => no linear LIV;
  quadratic scale ~ sqrt(24) E_P; margin ~6e8 vs GRB bounds; the
  chiral watch item. (Spin-off decision point: if the paper runs past
  ~30 pp, this becomes its own PRD note.)
- THE SCALARON: our own cancellation mechanism refuted in-house (the
  Bianchi reconstruction), then the scoping resolution — P7' exact in
  the (H -> 0, a -> 0) double idealization; two derived corrections
  (kappa-leak; sqrt(6) l_P scalaron), both quadratic, both
  sub-observable.
- THE SELECTOR CLOSURE: flat cells pass every metric gate identically;
  Finsler unstorable (DOF deficit 25 in 4D); direction dependence
  lives only at hinges, as curvature.
- THE BOUNDARY-CHANNEL KILL: the operator derived (constant pressure
  vs Casimir d^-4), floor-Newton-locked magnitude, excluded by 54-114
  orders; matter is strain content, not walls.
