# Is matter just a strain pattern in the same packing that makes the vacuum?

An exhibit, not a theorem: the obligations ledger lists "matter-as-defect" as gated physics, owing its own charter before it can claim anything — this node shows why the idea is live and what it still has to earn.

## The idea

If space is a frustrated tetrahedral packing (P10) and the vacuum's
floor energy is the cost of that frustration everywhere, the cheapest
next question is: what is a localized lump of extra strain? The
packing already has an answer sitting in its own ground-state ledger —
coordination defects.

- The ground state is not a single coordination number. Flatness forces
  a *mixture*: mostly n=5 hinges with a forced admixture of n=6
  (3D) or n=4/n=6 (4D), fixed by the deficit-angle identity, not
  chosen.
- Off-mixture coordination — a hinge that is n=4 or n=6 sitting where
  the mixture wants n=5 — costs strictly positive energy over the
  floor. The lab measured this directly, not just algebraically.
- The wedge-ring instrument (E2/E4) relaxed rings of n tetrahedra
  around a shared edge and found a strict energy minimum at n=5, with
  n=4 and n=6 rings relaxing to locally stable, higher-energy
  configurations:

```text
n = 4:  E = 0.061149
n = 5:  E = 0.000649   <- floor minimum
n = 6:  E = 0.052330
```

  positive, quantized by coordination, and requiring a discrete
  topological move to decay back to n=5.
- Bulk relaxation (051, the phase-2 lab) then found that these
  off-mixture hinges don't sit as scattered dust: the defect edges
  link up into a single spanning network through the bulk —
  disclination-type structure, not isolated points.

That is the whole candidate object, stated plainly: matter (or at
least a dark-sector candidate) = a persistent, gravitating pattern of
coordination defects riding on top of the frustration floor, held
together the same way the floor's mixture is held together — by the
same hinge energy that makes flatness cost anything at all. Nothing
about charge, spin, generations, or the Standard Model's matter
content is touched here; this is the crudest possible seed of a
"strain-mode unification," not a particle theory.

**Why it is gated, not adopted.** The obligations ledger is explicit:
this line item owes "its own charter, contract, kill conditions" and
enters "front door only" — the house rule that a mechanism may not be
adopted retroactively to explain data it wasn't built to predict. The
defect spectrum is measured (positive energy, quantized, networked);
what is *not* yet done is production (how defects come to exist),
abundance (how much of it there should be), clustering (whether the
network behaves like the dark-sector's observed structure), and a seat
in the actual dynamics. Until those are derived forward and given a
kill condition of their own, this stays an exhibit: here is the object
the packing offers, here is what it would have to do, here is nothing
claimed yet.
