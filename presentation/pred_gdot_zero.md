a falsifiable null forced by which of two indistinguishable readings of cosmic expansion actually funds itself

## The claim

VED commits to Gdot/G = 0, exactly, at every epoch — not "small," zero,
forever. This is register entry A6, and it did not start as a clean
prediction; it fell out of resolving an ambiguity the closed metric
sector could never settle on its own.

The setup: cosmic expansion can be read two ways at the level of the
packing. Either the graph is fixed and cells dilate (**stretch**:
l = l_0 a(t), N fixed), or the graph is fixed-scale and new cells are
added (**growth**: l = const, N ~ a^3). Both readings give the same
a^3 volume history and the same dust stress-energy — the metric sector
is blind to the difference, and always will be. The discriminator has
to come from each reading's side effects on quantities that are
otherwise locked.

Stretch dies twice, independently:

```text
floor-Newton lock:  G ~ 1/(f'(Delta_0) l)
stretch (l = l_0 a):  Gdot/G = -H ~ -7.2e-11/yr
LLR bound:           |Gdot/G| < 1.5e-13/yr
EXCLUDED, margin ~480x
```

and separately, stretch turns the sequestered vacuum floor into
Planck-density dust (wedge energies are dilation-invariant, so under
stretch the floor's constant energy sits in a growing volume, giving
p = 0 and a density that scales as a^-3 — no longer sequestered,
overclosing the universe by ~10^122). Either failure alone kills
stretch; both fire.

Growth survives cleanly: l stays constant, so G is pinned exactly,
and the floor's *measured* intensivity (u = const, from 051) gives
p = -u, i.e. w = -1 at the substance level — exactly the equation of
state that makes creating new cells energetically free (the first law
funds the creation term by term, no tuning). One reading, three locks
falling into place at once: the same constancy of l that keeps G fixed
is what makes creation self-funding.

The result is a hard commitment with no adjustable slack:

```text
Gdot/G = 0, exactly, at every epoch
```
