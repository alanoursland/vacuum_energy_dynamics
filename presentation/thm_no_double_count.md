# Does a falling body's kinetic energy get counted twice?

A reader who knows P6 (KE comes from real vacuum destroyed) and P10 (the packing axiom's closed-sector bookkeeping) needs to know these aren't two competing funding sources for the same infall — this theorem proves they're one.

## The theorem

Two postulates make claims about where a falling body's kinetic energy comes from, and on the surface they look like rivals. P6 says infall KE is funded by *substance destruction*: real vacuum content converted into kinetic energy as the body falls. Separately, the closed sector's own bookkeeping funds the same KE by *configuration deepening* — the cross-term in the two-body potential energy getting more negative as the bodies approach. If those are genuinely two different accounts, the theory double-counts energy at exactly the point P9's count-once fence is supposed to forbid.

The audit resolves this at the Newtonian anchor, exactly:

```text
U_cross = -G m1 m2 / d
KE(inf -> d) = +G m1 m2 / d
KE = -Delta(U_cross), zero residual, F = -dU_cross/dr pointwise
```

There is no room left for a second funding source. The identification is P1 itself — vacuum *is* energy, so "content below the frustration floor" and "destroyed vacuum" are the same fact under two names. P6's substance vocabulary and P10's configuration/strain vocabulary describe one ledger entry, not two: one event, two descriptions, the same resolution pattern P2 uses elsewhere in the theory.

The identification is quantified, not just asserted: the exterior field energy integrates to `-2GM^2/R` at leading order, matching the weak-field limit of the cap-ledger entry `-2GM^2/(R - r_s)` recorded elsewhere. Building a well of depth corresponding to mass M destroys `2GM^2/R` of vacuum — as strain content pushed below the floor, with no cell removed from the graph. P6's "destruction" is a content change, not graph surgery. The strong-field rate face (the exact Painlevé-Gullstrand river ledger) confirms the same identification away from the weak-field limit.

A second consistency check, P3 compatibility: the exchange is always in *amount*, never in *density*. The intensive, self-measured floor density stays protected under dilation invariance (rods are cells, so a dilation can't fake a density change); wells are extensive content deficits. The "volume-not-density" clause that P3 needs structurally is exactly what falls out of the identification — nothing has to be added by hand.

The seam holds at every anchor where both postulates make quantitative contact: Newtonian two-body, exact; static exterior, leading order; strong-field rate, via the PG ledger. It is not an accident that it closes — it's forced by P1's identity. What is *not* closed: the cosmological face. Whether cosmological expansion reads as graph growth (new cells created) or content rise within a fixed graph is left underdetermined by P10 as adopted — an open obligation (`p6_p10_cosmological_creation_045`), not a contradiction, assigned to the Lorentzian-dynamics program.
