# Where is matter?

The packing derives a vacuum energy sector and a gravitational law from geometry — but ordinary matter, the stuff of the standard model, is not in the theory. This node is about that gap.

## The claim

The vacuum sector is built entirely from the *undefected* frustrated tetrahedral packing: the mixed n=4/n=5 flat ground state, its floor energy, its coupling to curvature. Nowhere in that derivation chain does an electron, a quark, or a photon appear. What the packing *does* offer, unbidden, is coordination defects — disclination lines where the local wedge count departs from the ground mixture. Phase-2 bulk relaxation (derivation 051) found these defects form connected spanning networks, not isolated dust, and carry a positive, quantized energy spectrum monotone in the defect strength |delta|. That's a structure with the right shape to *be* matter — localized, energetic, topologically stable — but the theory does not yet claim it is.

The gated-physics table is explicit about the status:

```text
matter-as-defect | strain-mode unification | its own charter, contract, kill conditions | front door only
```

"Front door only" is the house rule: no candidate mechanism gets credit for what it might explain until it has its own charter — a written statement of what it predicts and what would kill it — checked by the forge like everything else. Backsolving from "we need matter to look like X" is explicitly forbidden (the same discipline that killed the non-gravitational-channels candidate in derivation 053). Matter-as-defect has not cleared that gate. No charter has been written; no kill conditions exist yet; no forge script has attempted a mass spectrum, a spin, a gauge charge, or a coupling constant from defect geometry.

The closest thing to a down payment is the dark-sector-excess line in the same table: coordination defects (from derivation 041's E4 class) are floated as a candidate for the dark-matter-like excess, but that candidate too "owes production, abundance, clustering, a seat in the dynamics" and is gated behind O-P10-5 (bulk relaxation) landing first. Phase 3 (periodic energy relaxation) is the prerequisite the theory itself names before any defect-as-particle claim can be evaluated quantitatively.

So the honest inventory: geometry gives you a vacuum, a cosmological constant mechanism, and linearized gravity. It gives you defects that *could* be matter's raw material. It does not give you the standard model, and the theory does not pretend otherwise.
