# What does this program not know yet?

A plain ledger of debts: obligations the adopted axiom P10 owes itself, and candidate physics still gated outside the theory.

## The claim

P10 is adopted, not finished. Two categories, kept strictly separate: **debts on P10** (things the theory must still derive to keep the axiom it already accepted) and **gated physics** (candidate claims that have not earned a front door in). Five debts, current as of derivations 047–054:

- **The packing scale `a` is undetermined.** `c_e = 36 arccos(1/3)/(√2 π) = 9.9743` edges per `a³` is pure geometry (derivation 052) — but the conversion factor `ρ_v = (c_e Δ₀/2) f'(Δ₀)/a³` has exactly one free length left, `a` itself. No derivation route exists; Planck-scale `a` is an assumption everywhere it's used. This is the same open question as "is space discrete."
- **The 4D ground coordination is resolved only at mean field** (derivation 050): flatness forces the mixture `x₄ = 5 − 2π/arccos(1/4) = 0.2332`, `x₅ = 0.7668`, mean `2π/arccos(1/4) = 4.7668` — parameter-free, and strictly positive (flatness is bought with frustration energy). What's unverified: whether this mixture is realizable in an actual periodic triangulation, not just an averaged claim.
- **The P7′ tension is closed, not dissolved** (derivations 047+048). The hoped-for cancellation was refuted in-house: the scalaron survives, mass `m² = 1/(6α)`, range `l* ~ √6 l_P ~ 4×10⁻³⁵ m` — 30 orders below the 54 μm laboratory frontier. "Exactly zero static flow" is now a scoped limit result, not an exact statement. The standing falsifier (F-P10-3, any gravitational-strength Yukawa at any range) stays open.
- **Lorentzian dynamics are narrowed but not solved** (derivation 054): the cosmological reading is decided — expansion is graph growth, not stretch (stretch killed twice), self-funded by the floor's `w = −1`, with `Ġ = 0` as a standing commitment. Still owed: the creation-suppression coefficient `α` in `C = 3H(1 − α δ)`, and the initial-value problem itself — the CDT-adjacent frontier.
- **Bulk relaxation is Phase 2 of 3** (derivation 051): floor intensivity confirmed, defect spectrum measured, disclination networks observed as a single spanning component (not dust), 4D coordination statistics land in the 050 window. Phase 3 — true periodic energy-gradient relaxation with retriangulation — is the piece that would turn "mean-field mixture" into a checked realizability verdict.

Three standing falsifiers sit under all of this regardless of how the debts resolve: a volume-mode restoring force appearing in the packing energy, the floor scale and the P1 conversion factor coming apart, or any P7′-class Yukawa/boundary-smoothing signal at any range. Any one kills P10 outright.

Gated physics — candidates that are not claims — include the dark-sector excess (coordination defects, still owing production/abundance/clustering), the interior compression cap, and matter-as-defect unification, each fenced behind its own charter. One gate closed hard: the engineered-boundary energy release (the founding hope of the whole program) was killed in derivation 053 — the instantiated operator is excluded by 54–114 orders of magnitude, and reopening it requires a front-door derivation of a specific suppressed coupling, not backsolving. Λ's numerical value is deliberately *not* on this list: the considered position is that it is a global datum, not the vacuum's to derive.
