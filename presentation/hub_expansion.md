# What is cosmic expansion, if space is a packing?

The trail from an underdetermined bookkeeping choice to a decided
theorem, two self-inflicted kills, a self-funding cosmology, and a new
standing falsifier — all from asking what "space growing" means for a
theory where space is a graph of frustrated tetrahedral cells.

## The trail

**1. The two readings.** Derivation 045 proved the packing postulate
(P6) and the expansion postulate (P10) mutually consistent but left
open *how* the packing expands. Two bookkeepings give identical volume
history $\propto a^3$ and source identical dust stress — the metric
sector cannot tell them apart, and never will:

```text
STRETCH   graph fixed, cells dilate: edge length ℓ(t) = ℓ₀ a(t), N const
GROWTH    scale fixed, cells created: ℓ = const, N ∝ a³
```

**2. Stretch dies twice.** Not because Einstein's equations disagree —
they can't — but because each reading has side effects on quantities
the theory had already locked elsewhere.

- *Kill 1:* the floor-Newton lock ties $G \propto 1/(f'(\Delta_0)\ell)$.
  Dilating $\ell$ with $a(t)$ forces $\dot G/G = -H \approx
  -7.2\times10^{-11}$/yr, against a lunar-laser-ranging bound of
  $1.5\times10^{-13}$/yr — excluded by a factor ~480.
- *Kill 2:* wedge energies are exactly dilation-invariant (measured
  many-body, derivation 041). Stretching keeps total floor energy fixed
  while volume grows, so $p = -dE/dV = 0$: the floor turns dustlike,
  diluting as $a^{-3}$ at Planck density. Sequestering (P3) only hides
  *constant* density, not diluting density — this would gravitate as
  dark matter at ~$10^{122}\times$ closure density, demolishing the
  sequestering architecture the Λ story depends on.

Both kills are independent; either alone is fatal.

**3. Growth is self-funding.** With $\ell$ fixed, $G$ is exactly
constant (no drift mechanism survives at all), and the floor's measured
intensivity ($E = uV$, $u$ constant, derivation 051) forces $p = -u$:
$w = -1$ exactly, term by term. That answers the old question of where
the energy for new space comes from — it's paid by the floor's own
negative-pressure work, $-p\,dV$, opening each new cubic meter. No
reservoir, no debt.

**4. The new falsifier.** Growth leaves no mechanism for $G$ drift —
not small, none. Register entry A6 commits to $\dot G/G = 0$ at every
epoch, killed by any confirmed nonzero drift. Current bounds sit only
~480× above $H_0$, and they keep tightening: this null is under live
experimental pressure, not a safe distance from it.

**5. The flow clause.** The founding intuition — that matter
constrains the local vacuum and expansion is pushed preferentially into
voids — gets a formal seat: $C = 3H(1-\alpha\delta)$. Since
$\langle\delta\rangle = 0$, the background is exactly $\langle C
\rangle = 3H$ for *any* $\alpha$, so the clause is invisible to every
observable claimed so far. $\alpha$ becomes computable only once
O-P10-4's Lorentzian dynamics exists — recorded as an open obligation,
not a claim.
