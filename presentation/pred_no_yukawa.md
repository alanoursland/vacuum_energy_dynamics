# Why does short-range gravity show no fifth force, ever?

Bench-top torsion balances and Casimir-adjacent experiments keep hunting for a Yukawa
deviation from Newton at the sub-millimeter scale — VED says they will keep finding nothing.

## The claim

Most modified-gravity and dark-energy rivals need a screened fifth force: a Yukawa
correction to Newtonian gravity that is *small* at long range (screened by density) but
would show up if you got close enough or dense enough. VED does not predict a small
deviation. It predicts zero, at every accessible range, full stop.

The mechanism: P7' (a structural constraint from the closed field-equation derivation)
kills the entire four-derivative sector, including every screened variant — chameleon,
symmetron, Vainshtein, chain-mediated, all of it (proof.md Theorems 6-7 plus the screening
corollary). There is no leftover four-derivative term that could seed a fifth force at
any range, screened or not.

Under P10 (the discrete-packing axiom adopted later in the program) there *is* an
R²-class term in the action, at a coefficient set by the Planck scale itself — this is
obligation O-P10-3, and it closed (derivations 047, 048). Closing it did two things:

- It rescoped P7' as the correct idealization in the continuum limit (a → 0): the
  four-derivative sector really does vanish there.
- It located the actual surviving scalaron: a real particle with range √6 · l_P ≈
  4×10⁻³⁵ m — about 30 orders of magnitude below the ~50 μm frontier of the best
  torsion-balance probes.

```text

current probe:   ~50 um (Eot-Wash-class torsion balances)
margin:          ~1e30 below the observational frontier
```

So the "zero" is not naive — it is a real particle sitting at a range so absurd it is
functionally unobservable at any density gravity experiments could reach. That is the
honest content of "zero": not "we haven't found the coupling yet," but "the theory places
it somewhere physics has no plans to ever probe."

ever independently measured by some other means, a scalaron Yukawa at range √6 · a becomes

That is the flip side of this null: the same mechanism that forbids a fifth force *now*
predicts one *later*, at a specific range, if the packing scale is ever pinned down.
