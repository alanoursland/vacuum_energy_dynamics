# When something falls, what actually pays for its kinetic energy?

The P6 pipeline: a falling body's kinetic energy is not manufactured from nothing — it is funded by real vacuum content, and the ledger for that exchange closes exactly, not at leading order.

## The idea

P6 was one of the ontology's founding intuitions — kinetic-energy
changes are funded by real vacuum exchange, not by an abstract
potential-energy bookkeeping trick. Its naive static form was killed
early (the v2 audit: a *static* configuration consumes nothing; see
[idea_frame_indifference.md](idea_frame_indifference.md)). What
survives is a pipeline, not a slogan:

```text
potential curvature -> (kinetic curvature, transient carrier) -> kinetic mass-energy
```

and in reverse, for an inspiral: kinetic mass-energy radiates away as
kinetic curvature at exactly the quadrupole rate. The two curvature
modes have opposite sector signs derived independently of any
packing — potential curvature is the elliptic, non-propagating,
NEGATIVE-energy constraint sector (u = −c⁴(s′)²/8πG, bound to its
sources); kinetic curvature is the hyperbolic, propagating,
POSITIVE-definite TT sector (K_T = c⁴/16πG). A gravity well is a
static strain redistribution; a gravitational wave is pure edge-length
motion in flight. Infall drains the first into the second and the
second into ordinary kinetic energy.

**The one-entry ledger.** The sharp danger this raises: P6 says infall
KE is funded by *substance destruction* (vacuum content dropping below
the floor); the closed sector's own bookkeeping funds the same KE by
*configuration deepening* (the cross-term in the field energy). Two
funding sources would double-count against the P9 count-once fence.
The audit (VacuumForge, derivation 045) proves they are one entry, not
two:

```text
U_cross = -G m1 m2 / d;  KE(inf -> d) = +G m1 m2 / d = -Delta U_cross,
zero residual, F = -dU_cross/dr pointwise.
```

The identification is P1 itself — vacuum IS energy, so "content below
the floor" and "configuration deepening" are two vocabularies for the
same event, exactly the pattern P2 uses to make vacuum-change and
spacetime-change one event. Quantified: the exterior field energy
integrates to −2GM²/R at leading order, matching the recorded
cap-ledger entry −2GM²/(R − r_s) in the weak-field limit. Building a
well destroys 2GM²/R of vacuum content — strain content pushed below
floor, with no cell removed from the graph. Exchange happens in
*amount*, never in *density*: the packing's intensive, dilation-invariant
density (P3) stays protected throughout: content moves, the ruler
doesn't.

**Exact, not asymptotic.** The strong-field face closes too. In the
Painlevé–Gullstrand slicing of the derived exterior (trial 021), the
exchange ledger reads ½v² = GM/r *at every radius*, with the horizon
sitting at exactly v = c — the causal bound saturating, not
approximating. This is why the Michell "coincidence" (the Newtonian
escape-velocity formula reproducing the Schwarzschild radius) and the
river-model "curiosity" aren't numerology in this theory: the P9
resummation is the reason the ledger closes at all orders, and the PG
picture is that closure made visible.

**The mechanism underneath.** Why does the vacuum fund infall at all,
at first order? Because it is pre-tensioned — the same f′(Δ₀) ≠ 0
condition that selects Einstein–Hilbert as the effective action in the
first place. Things fall because space is pre-tensioned; P6 is what
that pre-tension looks like from the falling body's side.

**What's still open, in the same breath.** The cosmological face —
whether expansion is graph growth (new cells) or content rise within a
fixed graph — is a genuine underdetermination in P10, not a
contradiction, parked in the Lorentzian-dynamics program. And the
speculative extension — matter itself as packing defects, which would
make E = mc² read as a defect's rest energy standing as strain in its
own core — is flagged, unlicensed, and deliberately kept out of this
ledger.
