# Do stars "burn" vacuum to power gravity?

An early (v2) picture had massive bodies continuously consuming the vacuum floor to source their field — the static ledger says no, and says it with a theorem, not a hunch.

## The kill

The v2 intuition: if matter is strain in a frustrated packing, and gravity is the packing's
response to that strain, maybe a static mass keeps *drawing down* vacuum around it — a
standing consumption current, like a star quietly burning fuel to keep its field topped up.
It's a natural picture once you take "matter deforms the vacuum" seriously, and it predicts
something extra: a non-cosmological energy flux threading every static exterior.

P7′ (Static Frame Indifference) rules this out directly. The postulate states that a strictly
static vacuum configuration carries no energy current and no preferred frame in the t-r
(temporal-radial) plane — nothing flows around a static constraint, full stop. In stress
language, conditional on the second-order divergence-free response of the probe's gates, this
is boost invariance of the effective stress:

```text
T^t_t = T^r_r
```

Trial C3 turns this into three theorems, not an assumption: (1) EQUIVALENCE — boost invariance
of the stress is exactly the metric shadow AB = const (no static current, no consumption
flow); (2) EXCLUSION — the specific move that would *produce* a consumption picture (placing
configuration energy explicitly as a scalar-type source term, which forces T^t_t − T^r_r =
−(φ′)²/B ≠ 0) breaks t-r boost invariance and is independently killed; (3) CONSISTENCY — the
conditional vacuum equations, run without assuming the answer, re-select the same static
exterior (λ = −1, Schwarzschild) that the consumption picture was invented to explain in the
first place. A star sitting still is not running a process. It holds a shape, and holding a
shape has no t-r current to hide a funding mechanism in.

The only currents the framework admits are P6's dynamic exchange regime (things that move) and
the substance-creation current tied to cosmic expansion (things that stretch) — both already
accounted for elsewhere, both distinct in kind from "a static star eating vacuum." The
consumption picture asked the static ledger to carry a term it structurally cannot hold.
