# What is the packing axiom?

P10 is the program's proposed microphysics for the vacuum, stated in full
in [`p10_packing_axiom.md`](../theory_v3/01_postulates/p10_packing_axiom.md).
It is an adopted structural postulate, not a measured fact: the evidence so
far is internal convergence, and the forward weight has to come from
pre-registered falsifiers: a detected volume-mode restoring force, the
floor scale and P1's conversion factor pulling apart, or a confirmed
gravitational-strength Yukawa deviation.  Each would kill the axiom; the
full inventory is the [prediction register](hub_predictions.md).

The configuration variable is an edge-length graph.  Vertices are the
discrete elements; edge lengths determine the local piecewise-flat metric.
In this reading, curvature is not assigned at every point of a smooth
manifold.  It lives on hinges as a [deficit angle](idea_deficit_angle.md),
which is standard Regge calculus.

The energy ansatz is local:

```text
E[X] = sum_h V_h f(delta_h),
```

with `V_h` the hinge measure and `f` a smooth per-hinge energy.  The model
file [`regge_delaunay_model.md`](../theory_v3/development/vacuum_sector/08_packing_microphysics/regge_delaunay_model.md)
is the working version of this statement.

The nonstandard move is the ground state.  Regular tetrahedra cannot tile
flat 3-space; five around an edge leave the fixed gap
[`Delta_0`](num_delta0.md).  P10 therefore says the vacuum ground state is
not "zero mismatch."  It is flat, but uniformly frustrated.

That one choice is what makes the axiom useful.  The constant part of the
energy becomes the [vacuum floor](idea_floor.md).  The first variation about
the frustrated point gives the [Einstein-Hilbert term](thm_expansion_point.md)
generically.  The same wedge stiffness then appears in both the floor and
Newton coupling, which is the floor-Newton lock checked in derivation 039.

The fence is as important as the claim.  P10 does not yet provide a quantum
theory, a continuum-limit proof, a Lorentzian dynamics, or a dark-matter
abundance calculation.  Those remain separate open obligations in the
[packing trail](hub_packing.md) and [open-problems trail](hub_open_problems.md).
