# If vacuum, energy, and spacetime are three names for one thing, what does that actually forbid?

The ontology in one page: two postulates, P1 and P2, chained into a single identity — and a warning about what the identity does *not* mean.

## The idea

Start from mass-energy equivalence, SR4. $E = mc^2$ isn't a conversion factor between two different things; mass and energy are the same quantity in different clothes. The framework's foundational move (P1) is to run the same identification on the vacuum: **vacuum is energy**, not "contains" or "carries" energy. Wherever there is vacuum, there is energy, full stop — the amount of vacuum in a region and the energy stored as vacuum in that region are the same number, related by some conversion factor analogous to $c^2$ (its value is open — see below).

That alone would make vacuum a substance with energy content instead of an absence. The second postulate (P2) goes further and asks what vacuum *is* in relation to spacetime — defined functionally as whatever structure constrains how information (signals, forces, correlations) can propagate. Four options exist: vacuum as a field on a separate spacetime; vacuum coupled to spacetime by some mediating dynamics; vacuum identical to spacetime; spacetime emergent from a deeper vacuum substructure. The framework adopts the third:

```text
P2: vacuum ≡ spacetime — one substance, two angles of description
```

Chain P1 and P2 and the corollary is forced: **spacetime is energy**, literally, in the SR4 sense, not as a metaphor for "spacetime has an energy density" or "spacetime couples to a stress-energy tensor" (both of those are already claims ordinary GR makes and require none of this). The stronger claim is that there is no spacetime *in addition to* energy. A vacuum-energy exchange and a spacetime change are one event described twice, not two events connected by a coupling constant, a mediation delay, or a separate equation of motion for the geometric response.

Why adopt identity over coupling? Economy. Options 1 and 2 require importing Einstein's field equations wholesale or inventing a coupling with free parameters to pin down later. Option 4 requires a vacuum substructure the framework doesn't have. Option 3 removes an entire layer of possible free parameters in one move — no coupling to be strong or weak, no lag, no separate dynamics to solve. This is the postulate doing real work, not renaming: it makes the framework more falsifiable, not less, because there's nowhere left to hide a fudge factor.

**A direct consequence:** spacetime is not conserved. When a process destroys vacuum — converts vacuum energy into some other form, e.g. kinetic energy of a falling body — it also destroys spacetime at that location; there is less spacetime there than before. When cosmic expansion creates vacuum, it creates spacetime. Total spacetime in a region is a dynamical quantity, not a fixed backdrop.

**What "substance" does NOT mean here.** It does not mean the vacuum is extractable energy sitting around for the taking. The vacuum sits at its own minimum-energy configuration everywhere unconstrained; there's no lower state for it to fall to, so nothing can be harvested from it by the usual high-state-to-low-state extraction trick. The only way to get energy *out* of vacuum is to actually consume vacuum substance itself — destroy some of it — not rearrange it, not lower its density (P3 forbids that), just consume it. "Substance" also does not mean a scalar field bolted onto GR's metric, and does not mean a return to 19th-century aether: the identity is with spacetime's information-propagation role, stated functionally, not with a preferred rest frame or a medium for wave propagation in the old sense.
