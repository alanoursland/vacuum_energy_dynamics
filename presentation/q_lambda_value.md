# What fixes Λ's actual value?

A reader who has just been told Λ is a global datum, not a local quantity, wants to know: so what does fix it — and is that a gap or a closed case?

## The claim

Nothing in the vacuum sector fixes Λ's value, and the theory's considered position is that nothing *should*: the value is external, and three independent structures converge on why.

- **It is structurally an integration constant, not a coupling.** Under the unimodular constraint (P3, the volume mode frozen because the packing gives it no restoring force), the Bianchi identity plus matter conservation force
  ```text
  R + (8*pi*G/c^4)*T = 4*Lambda = const
  ```
  Λ enters the field equations here — fixed by a conservation law, not placed in the action. No local variation can ever touch it (forge 033–034).

- **It is a curvature datum, not an energy datum.** Since that combination is constant along the motion and the floor's density sequesters out of it in a vacuum epoch, $\Lambda = \tfrac14 R_{\text{ground}}$ — the intrinsic curvature of the vacuum's ground configuration. This is *why* the floor's frustration energy density does not set Λ: geometry and energy have already been separated (036).

- **The magnitude channel is closed, not open.** The exact relief function $\cos\delta(s) = \cos s/(1+2\cos s)$ says spherical curvature relieves frustration and hyperbolic curvature aggravates it, so *if* the ground state curved to relieve frustration it would curve with Λ ≥ 0 — but the relief is quadratic-flat ($\Delta(s) = \Delta_0 - \tfrac{5\sqrt2}{24}s^2 + O(s^4)$). Matching the observed Λ this way relieves ~$10^{-122}$ of the frustration — indistinguishable from none — while any curvature large enough to matter overshoots by the same ~122 orders of magnitude in the other direction. No intermediate regime exists (037). The theory's actual branch: a flat ground state, Λ_ground = 0, frustration retained and sequestered.

- **The Henneaux–Teitelboim reading makes the externality precise.** Λ is the variable conjugate to the total four-volume — a boundary/initial condition of the universe, on the same footing as "the universe's total mass." The theory commits to deriving every *local* quantity; Λ is provably not one, so demanding a derivation of it is demanding the wrong kind of answer (035, 037).

This closes the *why* — six independent local-selector routes (variational, boundary, topology, measure, relaxation, frustration-microphysics; derivations 008–016) each failed to value Λ without importing an external scale, and that failure is now explained rather than merely observed: it's the defining behavior of an integration constant. What stays open, honestly, is the live remainder next to Λ: the F1 leak, a matter-era violation of the unimodular constraint, $AB - 1 = \tfrac32\Omega_m(H_0r/c)^2 \approx 6\times10^{-31}$ at 1 AU (034) — a derived honesty entry, not a target, and not a route back to Λ's value.
