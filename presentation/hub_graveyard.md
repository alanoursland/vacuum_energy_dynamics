# What has this program killed with its own hands?

If you doubt motivated reasoning, start here — not with the claims, with the corpses.

## The claim

The house rule is kill-condition-before-claim: every mechanism gets a falsification test
before it gets to be a result, and an executed kill is written up with the same care as a
success. Four major graves, each with a verified death certificate:

**The partial-relief route to Λ** (killed 2026-06, derivation 037). The hope: the frustrated
ground state relieves a sliver of its strain as residual curvature, and that sliver is the
observed cosmological constant. The kill: the relief function is exact and computable —

```text
cos δ(s) = cos s / (1 + 2cos s),  Δ(s) = Δ₀ − (5√2/24)s²
```

— quadratically flat about the ground state. Matching the observed Λ would require relieving
~10⁻¹²² of the frustration, and nothing selects that particular sliver. The value-from-floor
route to Λ is dead, permanently.

**Our own scalaron-cancellation mechanism** (killed 2026-07-02, derivation 047). The packing's
R²-class correction looked like it might violate postulate P7′ ("four-derivative sector
exactly empty"); the in-house hope was that the unimodular constraint kills the scalaron for
free. Also in-house, same week: the f(R) field-equation tensor is identically conserved, so
the Bianchi identity reconstructs the trace equation from the unimodular system — the same
mechanism that makes Λ an integration constant *resurrects* the scalaron, mass m² = 1/(6α),
intact. Proposed and refuted by the same people in the same week. The real tension was
resolved by a scoping ruling instead: P7′ exact in the continuum idealization, the
Planck-range scalaron a controlled correction ~10³⁰ below laboratory reach.

**The engineered-boundary energy release** (killed 2026-07-02, derivation 053) — the founding
dream of the whole program: wall off matter into boundaries that confine the vacuum packing
into quasi-2D (triangles tile perfectly in 2D, no frustration), let the confined region relax
below the 3D floor, and tap the released energy Casimir-style. The kill: once the operator
became derivable with no free coefficient, the same lock that calibrates Newton's G fixes the
released pressure at ~10¹¹² Pa. Casimir-class experiments bound anomalous plate pressures at
~10⁻² Pa. Excluded by 54–114 orders of magnitude. Matter cannot wall off the vacuum —
consistent with matter *being* strain in the packing, not a container for it. It died of
magnitude, not thermodynamics (a closed cycle nets exactly zero regardless).

**The stretch reading of expansion** (killed 2026-07-02, derivation 054). The candidate: cosmic
expansion is a fixed vacuum graph whose cells dilate with the scale factor. Killed twice over:
the microstructural length calibrates G, so stretching forces Ġ/G = −H, excluded ~480× by
lunar laser ranging; and dilation-invariant cell energies leave the stretched floor
pressureless at Planck density, unprotected by sequestering, overclosing the universe by
~10¹²². Space grows by adding cells; it does not stretch existing ones.

Also buried, more briefly: Finsler-inside-the-metric (the edge-length state space stores
*exactly* a metric per cell — 10 edges = 10 components in 4D, a quartic norm needs 35; not
suppressed, unstorable — derivation 049); every pre-packing route to nonzero Λ (a nine-
derivation sweep, 008–016, all negative, later understood as theorems of the unimodular
reading); scalar and vector gravitational radiation (killed in the field-equation closure —
only the two tensor polarizations survive); "the floor gravitates as dark energy" (sequestering
forbids it — the floor contributes exactly zero).

Every one of these was the program's own idea, and the boundary-release mechanism was the
reason this research started at all. They died because the kill conditions were set before the
answers came in — the same rules that currently expose the theory's live claims to
observation.
