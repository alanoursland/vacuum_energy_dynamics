# Why does a static vacuum carry no energy current?

P7′ is the postulate behind $AB=1$: a settled configuration has no flow, and "no flow" turns out to be a limit, not an absolute.

## The claim

A strictly static vacuum configuration carries no energy current and picks out no preferred frame in the temporal-radial plane. Nothing flows around a static constraint, and nothing in the t-r plane distinguishes one boost from another. Flow only happens when something moves (P6's exchange regime) or when the cosmos stretches (substance creation). A static configuration is pure shape — holding a form is not a process.

In stress language, conditional on the second-order divergence-free gate structure, this is boost invariance of the effective stress:

```text
T^t_t = T^r_r
```

By the Trial C3 identity relating this to the Einstein-tensor difference, its metric shadow in a static spherical exterior is $A(r)B(r) = \text{const} = 1$ with asymptotic flatness. Old P7 (the metric-level statement physicists actually use) is recovered as a *consequence* of P7′, not asserted separately — this is what let P7′ replace P7 as the primitive commitment in 2026.

With P9 (configuration energy gravitates) and P7′ (static frame indifference) both adopted, nothing in the postulate set was reverse-engineered to match GR after the fact — P8 falls out as a 1PN theorem, P7 falls out as a metric shadow. **The "exactly zero" problem.** No real exterior is strictly static. Every mass sits in an expanding cosmos, and — as of derivation 048 — every mass is also built from a discrete packing. Both facts inject small currents that "exactly zero" can't literally have. So P7′ is adopted as a *double idealization*, exact in two limits at once:

```text
H -> 0   (no cosmic expansion: the pure-Lambda case is exact, not approximate)
a -> 0   (continuum limit of the packing: no discreteness)
```

Two derived, registered corrections currently exist:

- **The κ-leak (Trial F1, expansion):** under pure-Λ expansion the shadow is exact — Schwarzschild-de Sitter has AB=1 identically, P7′ is *stronger* than first scoped. The surviving deviation is matter-sourced and second order: AB − 1 = (3/2)Ω_m(H₀r/c)² ≈ 5.6×10⁻³¹ at 1 AU. Derived, parameter-free, unobservable at solar-system scale; the linear-scaling estimate once floated here is superseded.
- **The packing scalaron (derivation 047/048):** the unimodular constraint does not cancel the packing's R²-class term; the Bianchi mechanism reconstructs a scalaron with m² = 1/(6α), giving a Compton length ℓ* = √(6α) ≈ 4×10⁻³⁵ m at Planck-scale packing — thirty orders of magnitude below the 54 μm laboratory frontier.

Both corrections are quadratic in their small parameter, sub-observable, and close no previously-open coefficient. The official reading: **"no static flow, exactly zero" is a limit result** — exactness belongs to the idealization, and the physical vacuum's deviations from it must each be derived, sized, and logged, not waved away. The register currently holds exactly these two entries.
