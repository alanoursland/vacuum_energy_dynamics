# Where is quantum mechanics?

The whole chain — packing, floor energy, field equations, Λ — is derived classically; this node is about that absence.

## The claim

Every result in the theory to date is a classical stationarity statement. The packing relaxes to a ground state by minimizing a classical energy functional; the field equations come from varying a classical action (`δS = 0`); the floor density, the mixture fractions, the scalaron mass — all of it falls out of geometry and calculus, not operators. Nowhere does `hbar` enter. The house's own audit says this plainly: classical metric-stress variation "contains no quantization scale by itself," and quantization, Hilbert-space structure, and commutators are "not derived by the classical stress route." That's not a gap being politely elided — it's logged as a named obligation the theory does not yet own.

What "quantum mechanics" would even mean for a discrete packing is itself unstarted work, not a stated program:

- No canonical or path-integral quantization of the tetrahedral packing's degrees of freedom exists. The packing scale `a` (Planck-scale, by assumption — see the packing-scale node) is the natural place a quantum theory would live, but no derivation route to `a` exists classically or quantum-mechanically.
- The Lorentzian dynamics obligation (O-P10-4) is explicitly classical in scope: an initial-value formulation of the packing's *classical* evolution, CDT-adjacent in spirit. Causal Dynamical Triangulations is itself a quantum-gravity research program built on triangulated geometries — the natural neighbor to watch — but nothing in VED has yet borrowed its path-integral machinery.
- Matter — the sector that would normally carry quantum numbers, spin-statistics, and Hilbert-space structure — is itself gated behind its own unopened charter (see "Where is matter?"). Quantizing a theory that doesn't yet have matter fields is a second, compounding absence.

So the honest inventory: this is a classical discrete-geometry theory of gravity and vacuum energy, full stop, as of now. It recovers GR's weak-field limit, gets a cosmological constant from substance properties, and predicts a scalaron — all classically. Whether the packing has an underlying quantum description, whether `a` is a quantization scale, whether spacetime discreteness at that scale implies anything about decoherence or a UV completion — none of this has been touched. The theory does not claim quantum gravity. It claims classical discrete gravity with a derived Λ, and says so.
