## The Question

The vacuum is a physical substance whose configuration supplies
spacetime geometry. At the microscopic level, the model's vertices are
**spatial quanta with Delaunay adjacency**. Edges carry lengths, and
those lengths fix the geometry of each tetrahedron. The tetrahedra are
how the model represents the geometry among neighboring quanta
([P2](../../../01_postulates/p2_vacuum_spacetime_identity.md),
[P10](../../../01_postulates/p10_packing_axiom.md)).

The research question:

```text
At fixed macroscopic metric, does a periodic, non-inverting
configuration generated by VED's strain model leave a reproducible
directional signature in Maxwell modes after averaging?
```

Both outcomes are informative.

- **A signal** would mean the arrangement of spatial quanta carries
  directional information that survives averaging and reaches an
  electromagnetic mode.
- **A null** would show how local angle-gap strain can store energy
  while the modeled large-scale optical response stays isotropic.

## Two Quantities to Keep Distinct

```text
Delta_0  = 2 pi - 5 arccos(1/3) ~ 7.36 deg
           the mismatch of the PREFERRED arrangement: five regular
           Euclidean tetrahedra around an edge leave this gap

delta_h  = 2 pi - (sum of the ACTUAL incident dihedral angles at h)
           the Regge deficit of an actual configuration
```

- On the regular 600-cell, the cells are regular, so delta_h = Delta_0
  at every hinge. Curvature absorbs the gap and there is no strain.
- In a valid flat periodic embedding, delta_h = 0 at every interior
  edge, yet the configuration can carry positive strain energy. That
  energy is the cost of the quanta departing from their preferred
  geometry so that the arrangement closes.

Code must compute these two quantities separately and must never
report one under the name of the other. A strain variable built only
from delta_h reads zero in a flat configuration however strained it is.

## What Maxwell Is Here

Discrete Maxwell theory is placed on the edge geometry as a test field:

```text
E  1-cochain on edges        B  2-cochain on faces
d1^T *2 d1 e = (omega/c)^2 *1 e
```

The Hodge stars `*1`, `*2` carry all of the geometric input. That
makes them the place where an interpretive choice enters the probe.

### The Hodge-star fork (`hodge_star_interpretive_fork`)

```text
(a) actual-geometry star   circumcentric (signed) or Whitney weights
                           built from the actual edge lengths.
                           The default. It probes whether the actual
                           arrangement of quanta leaves a signature.

(b) mismatch-dependent     a constitutive star that depends on the
    star                   preferred-vs-actual mismatch. A VED
                           interpretive choice, not defined here; the
                           theory owner's to make.
```

The expected property of (a): uniform E and B are exact discrete
static solutions on any valid embedded mesh with signed circumcentric
weights.

- Gauss: d0^T *1 e at a vertex is E dotted into the closed dual cell's
  area vectors.
- Faraday dual: d1^T *2 b at an edge is B dotted into the closed loop
  of circumcenters around it.

So under (a) the long-wavelength rank-2 response is the macroscopic
metric, and any signature must appear at finite k. This is recorded as
an **expected property to verify** (gate G3 below), not as a result.
It is a property of choice (a). A null obtained under (a) is not a
verdict on VED.

## Stages

### Stage 1 — `maxwell_regular_mesh_reproduction_056`

Port the scratch calculation in
[regular_mesh_benchmark.md](regular_mesh_benchmark.md) into the forge
as derivation 056. Acceptance checks:

```text
counts     upstairs 120 / 720 / 1200 / 600; quotient 1 / 6 / 10 / 5
action     I* acts on the LEFT; edge class = Im(conj(q_i) q_j)
           (invariant under simultaneous left multiplication)
smooth     omega R / c = 2 (analytic; the curl sign -2/R is a
           manuscript input, not tested by the discrete operator)
discrete   lowest triplet omega R / c = 2.10292445
           second triplet omega R / c = 8.90813092
pattern    the sampled frame patterns span the lowest eigenspace
symmetry   the A5 character of the signed edge representation is
           (6, -2, 0, 1, 1) = chi_3 + chi_3'
control    a 1% stretch of one edge class splits the low triplet
           (reference: 2.08334853 x2, 2.13210754)
```

The quotient is a one-vertex triangulation with face identifications.
All six edges are loops, which an ordinary abstract simplicial-complex
data structure cannot represent. The scratch code works upstairs on
all 720 edges and classes them by `Im(conj(q_i) q_j)`. The forge
version should either do the same or use a cell-complex structure that
allows loops.

### Stage 2 — `periodic_noninverting_spring_control`

An engineering question: can a valid mesh be maintained at fixed box
metric, and do stable Maxwell modes come out of it?

```text
energy        the 051 spring objective E = sum_e (l_e - 1)^2
box           periodic, FIXED cell matrix (the "fixed macroscopic
              metric"); affine stationarity no longer forces zero
              stress, so the virial tensor W becomes a measurement
validity      positive oriented volumes, enforced by a documented
              barrier or constrained solver
record        minimum signed volume; barrier sensitivity; W;
              minimum signed *1 and *2 weights; the gates below
modes         omega(k) along many directions, tracked against ka;
              degeneracy splitting; averages over seeds
```

What to watch: whether degeneracies survive, whether frequencies
shift, and which changes persist after averaging.

### Stage 3 — `p10_wedge_strain_definition`, then the P10 hinge run

This stage answers the VED question with the P10 energy rather than the
spring proxy. Before any bulk run:

1. Write down how the preferred angle gap Delta_0 produces a stored
   deformation in the chosen f. In a periodic flat embedding the actual
   deficit is zero at every valid interior edge, so f must see the
   mismatch through the actual cell geometry, not through delta_h
   alone.
2. Verify it on a single five-tetrahedron wedge. The spring-model
   reference is 041 E2: the relaxed n = 5 ring has E = 0.000649.

Only then go to bulk. Otherwise stages 2 and 3 could differ for an
accidental reason. The choice of f is the theory owner's.

```text
                 entry makes the generalized eigenproblem indefinite,
                 so either keep the mesh Delaunay, retriangulate, or
                 switch to Whitney mass matrices
G5  controls     separate a genuine signature from (i) finite-sample
                 fluctuation (vary N and seed) and (ii) the periodic
                 cell's own symmetry (vary the cell shape, or correlate
                 with an orientational order parameter of the packing)
```

G5(ii) links the probe to 051's open phase 3
(`periodic_energy_relaxation_051`). Whether the relaxed packing develops
orientational order is a question both programs need answered.

## Fences

```text
- Maxwell is a probe; no claim that spatial quanta produce EM.
- No observable channel is proposed; 06 quarantine applies to any
  future one.
- The discrete operator measures omega^2; it cannot distinguish the
  two curl signs. Helicity is a manuscript input.
- One regular mesh is a benchmark, not a convergence result.
- The scratch Hodge assembly uses unsigned circumcentric distances,
  valid only near regular tetrahedra; do not reuse it on relaxed
  meshes.
```
